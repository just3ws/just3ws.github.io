# The Sound Above: Series Introduction and Support

**Format:** Solo series update
**Purpose:** Explain what The Sound Above is, what Mike is planning, and how viewers can follow or support the work.
**Target length:** 8 to 12 minutes, flexible
**Status:** Recording draft. Patreon call to action must match the page status when the video is published.

## 1. Open with the reason

**Scene:** Host camera. Start conversationally.

I have been thinking about what it means to return to an old conversation.

For years, I recorded interviews with people in the software community. Some of those conversations are more than a decade old. I want to revisit them and hear what is still useful now.

That is the idea behind The Sound Above.

This is a chance to explain what I am making, what I hope to explore, and how you can follow along.

**Freeform prompt:** What brought this series back to mind now? Use a specific moment, archive task, or question that is true today.

## 2. Explain the series

**Scene:** Host camera. Archive page or interview still is optional.

The Sound Above is a series about revisiting conversations from the UGtastic and WHOIS Tech Community interview archive.

I want to listen to what people said in their own words, put the recording back in its time and place, and ask what those ideas mean to people building software today.

The archive is the source. My memories and present-day reactions are part of the conversation, but they are not a substitute for what the guest said.

**Freeform prompt:** Describe one thing you noticed while working with the archive. Avoid naming a guest or making a historical claim unless you can point to its source.

## 3. Say why the work matters to you

**Scene:** Host camera. Keep this personal and specific.

I care about these recordings because technical communities are made by people showing up for each other. The interviews preserve some of those moments: what people were trying, how they learned, and how they made room for someone else to participate.

I also want to see what I remember clearly and what the recording helps me notice again. That can be a useful kind of history. It leaves room for correction and for other people to remember the same time differently.

**Freeform prompt:** Share one memory that belongs to you. Mark it as your recollection. Leave space for the guest's account and other people's memories.

## 4. Describe the plan without promising a schedule

**Scene:** Host camera. A simple season outline may appear on screen.

I have a working Season One target of seventeen interviews. The first one in the current sequence is my conversation with Sergio Pereira about Chicago Alt.NET.

That is a planning target, not a release schedule. I want to make the first episodes, learn what works, and adjust the format as I go.

The episodes can connect an interview to its community, conference, and time. Some may include transcripts or other archive material. I will decide what belongs in each one as I prepare it.

**Freeform prompt:** Name the next topic only if the sequence is still current when recording. Do not promise a date or cadence.

## 5. Invite viewers into the conversation

**Scene:** Host camera. Invite a concrete response.

If you were part of one of these communities, I would like to hear what you remember. Which room, meetup, conversation, or person helped you find your place in software?

You can leave a comment. If you remember a detail differently, say so. A specific memory or a source link will help me follow up carefully.

I am building this as a public record. I want it to be useful to people who were there and to people discovering these stories now.

## 6. Invite people to follow or support the work

**Scene:** Host camera. Keep the invitation direct and low pressure.

The simplest way to follow the series is to subscribe to the YouTube channel. That is free, and it helps you find the next video when it is ready.

Patreon is one way to support the time I spend recording, editing, caring for the interview archive, and making software. I share occasional notes about that work and host our Hallway community chat. I do not have a fixed posting schedule, and membership will not unlock private recordings or change what I choose to make public.

If you would like to support the work, the Patreon link is in the description. Please join only if the offer makes sense for you. The public videos and interviews will remain available on their usual sites.

**Production check:** The Patreon creator page is published at https://www.patreon.com/c/just3ws with the $5 Studio Notes tier and Hallway community chatroom active.

## 7. Close

**Scene:** Host camera. No forced sign-off needed.

That is what I am trying to make with The Sound Above: a way to listen again, add context carefully, and keep these conversations available to people who care about them.

Subscribe if you would like to follow along. And if you were part of one of these communities, tell me what you remember.

Thanks for spending some time with me. I will see you in the next one.

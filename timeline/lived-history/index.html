How to read the dates, sources, and photographs

These entries combine my recollections, photographs, and public records. A source note explains the basis of a claim; it does not mean every detail has independent confirmation.

A photograph's capture date, an event date, and a later publication date can differ. Approximate and unknown dates stay labeled. Their place in this reading order does not establish a precise date.

Gallery captions currently describe the event as a whole. Individual image descriptions and identifications are still being developed. Open a photograph to view its larger web copy.

The selections are my own history to tell. A person appearing in a photograph is not an endorsement of this site or its projects.

Sergio Pereira, Chicago Alt.NET & The SCNA Context

"The first user group I ever went to was Chicago Alt.NET. And the first user group I ever spoke at was Chicago Alt.NET. That's why I wanted to interview you first." (Mike Hall to Sergio Pereira at SCNA 2011)

Meeting on the 2nd Wednesday of each month on the 50th floor of the Sears Tower at Redpoint Technologies, Sergio Pereira created the template for cross-platform peer learning in Chicago. By bringing TDD, BDD, and open-source practices to an enterprise .NET town, Alt.NET laid the groundwork for everything that followed: BDI, 8th Light's Pair Friday, SCNA, CDG, and SCMC.

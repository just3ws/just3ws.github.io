UGtastic story · 2011–2026

The question this timeline answers

How did UGtastic grow from community conversations into an archive that could be preserved, rediscovered, and understood years later?

UGtastic began with community and conference interviews in 2011. Recording expanded through 2015. Later work preserved captions and files, followed by a return to transcription, indexing, and curation. The timeline separates when conversations happened from when their files were later processed.

How to read the marks
  • Event: a dated occurrence or group of recordings.
  • Active span: work supported across a stated interval.
  • Approximate: the source supports a year or range, not an exact day.
  • Unverified recollection: useful context that still needs independent support.
  • Quiet space: no dated archive signal for that lane. It does not prove nothing happened.

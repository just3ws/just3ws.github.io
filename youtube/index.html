Platform lineage

Vimeo first, YouTube now

The early recordings used Vimeo because YouTube's licensing and platform conditions were difficult to work with at the time. The original platform choice is part of the archive's history. YouTube is a later public distribution and synchronization surface, while the site keeps the transcript, provenance, and platform relationships together.

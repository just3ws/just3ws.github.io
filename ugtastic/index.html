01 · Notice the mark

Logos, marks, avatars

Start with the mark. Before there was a catalog, there was a recognizable way to say, “this conversation belongs here.” These recovered assets are shown as artifacts from the changing identity, not as a claim that one version replaced another on a precise date.

04 · Keep the honest gap

Episode music notes are gated

Raw WITC and Vimeo media are not used to assign intro or outro labels here unless the file is first confirmed identical to the published interview. The SoundCloud tracks remain available as public references. Episode-level music notes will appear after that identity check.

This keeps source identity, copyright, and publication provenance separate. No raw interview audio is republished by this exhibit.

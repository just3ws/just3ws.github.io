The IronLanguages Podcast 2010 Retrospective

Exploring dynamic languages on the CLR, open-source tooling, and the bridge to the software craftsmanship movement.

In early 2010, before UGtastic video interviews and before SCMC meetings were recorded at Follett, Mike Hall launched The IronLanguages Podcast (ironlanguages.net). The show chronicled the emergence of dynamic languages, meta-programming, and open-source package management on the Common Language Runtime.

{% include public-archive-note.html %}
Historical Provenance & Origin
Broadcasting Era: June 2010 to October 2010
Original Domain: ironlanguages.net (Posterous / Wayback Machine)
Focus: Dynamic Language Runtime (DLR), IronRuby, IronPython, Chocolatey/Nu Package Management, and Polyglot Architecture.
Archival Status: Republished and indexed within the just3ws career datalake.

The Bridge to Software Craftsmanship

The IronLanguages project was a critical inflection point. Exploring IronRuby and dynamic dispatch exposed developers to Ruby's expressive syntax, meta-programming, and rapid Test-Driven Development cycles while maintaining interop with existing enterprise .NET runtimes.

That journey spurred deeper involvement in Chicago ALT.NET, prompted joining Obtiva in August 2010, and led directly to co-founding Software Craftsmanship McHenry County (SCMC) to bring rigorous automated testing and polyglot practices to suburban engineers.

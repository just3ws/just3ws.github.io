# Sponsorship & Supporting Open Forensic Craftsmanship

## The Mission

The work conducted in `just3ws/dehum` and documented across [*The Sound Above*](https://www.just3ws.com) is dedicated to:
1. **Public, Unvarnished Forensic Auditing**: Examining what AI models actually generate vs. what they claim to do.
2. **Chicago Software Craftsmanship**: Reviving the disciplines of rigorous testing, simple design, and deep domain mastery in an age of automated code generation.
3. **Physical Ground Truth**: Demanding that automated tools remain anchored in acoustic, mathematical, and mechanical reality.

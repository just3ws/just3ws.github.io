The Operating Sequence: Stabilize • Understand • Improve

When entering a complex production system, legacy codebase, or founder transition, technical decision-making requires empirical evidence rather than guesswork. System Cartography is the empirical architecture mapping discipline of the Panoramic View methodology—reconciling stakeholder mental models with actual runtime behavior.

1. STABILIZE

Establish safe delivery boundaries, protect clinical and business operations, and contain immediate operational risk.

2. UNDERSTAND

Trace interactions from browser to database, map lateral state dependencies, and build a shared, evidence-backed model.

3. IMPROVE

Modernize with confidence, eliminate outage cascades, and empower engineering teams with sustainable architecture.

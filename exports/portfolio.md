# Project Portfolio

## Technical Conversation Archive
**Role:** Curator & Forensic Engineer
**Years:** 2006 - 2026

A high-fidelity restoration of 184 indexed technical interviews across 212 recorded
assets from the Ruby, JVM, and Software Craftsmanship movements. I used local AI
(Whisper/LLM) to perform forensic transcription, dialogue restructuring, and SEO
optimization to preserve technical memory across a changing industry.

## Phalanx Duel
**Role:** Creator & Architect
**Years:** 2024 - Present

Tactical card combat built around a familiar 52-card vocabulary, compact formations, and
replayable rules. A hands-on laboratory for rules engines, observable state transitions,
and product architecture.

## WWWorkRemote
**Role:** Principal Architect
**Years:** 2021 - Present

A local-first, human-supervised job search system for ingesting, searching, ranking,
triaging, and advancing job leads through applications and interview preparation.

## Chicago Code Camp
**Role:** Volunteer & Web Developer
**Years:** 2010 - 2012

An established community conference in the Chicago area. I volunteered and assisted
for a couple years, designing and developing the official event websites and speaker
management systems for at least three conferences.

## UGtastic
**Role:** Founder & Host
**Years:** 2012 - 2014

A technical media platform dedicated to capturing high-bandwidth technical
conversations. Produced over 200 interviews with industry leaders at the start of the
DevOps and Craftsmanship movements.

## UGl.st
**Role:** Creator
**Years:** 2014

The User-Group List. I built this project to help user-group organizers promote their
communities and bridge the gap between physical conference interactions and digital
discovery. Features the signature heart glyph in the user-group registration interface,
with the initial capture on January 11, 2014 and the final snapshot under my ownership
on December 22, 2014.

# Project Portfolio

## Technical Conversation Archive
**Role:** Curator & Forensic Engineer
**Years:** 2006 - 2026

A high-fidelity restoration of 214 technical interviews from the Ruby, JVM, and Software Craftsmanship movements. I used local AI (Whisper/LLM) to perform forensic transcription, dialogue restructuring, and SEO optimization to preserve technical memory across a changing industry.

## Phalanx Duel
**Role:** Creator & Architect
**Years:** 2024 - Present

Tactical card combat built around a familiar 52-card vocabulary, compact formations, and replayable rules. A hands-on laboratory for rules engines, observable state transitions, and product architecture.

## Chicago Code Camp
**Role:** Co-Organizer & Web Lead
**Years:** 2010 - 2012

A community-focused code camp in the Chicago area. I served as a co-organizer and designed and developed the official event websites and speaker management systems for the 2010-2012 seasons.

## UGtastic
**Role:** Founder & Host
**Years:** 2012 - 2014

A technical media platform dedicated to capturing high-bandwidth technical conversations. Produced over 200 interviews with industry leaders at the start of the DevOps and Craftsmanship movements.

## UGl.st
**Role:** Creator
**Years:** 2014

The User-Group List. I built this project to help User-Group organizers promote their communities and bridge the gap between physical conference interactions and digital discovery.

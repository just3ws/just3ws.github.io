Privacy and measurement

This site is a public portfolio and historical archive. I aim to learn which material helps people without turning visitors into profiles.

Analytics

The site uses GoatCounter for privacy-conscious, aggregate visit measurement. It does not use advertising profiles, persistent tracking cookies, local storage, or stored IP addresses according to its published privacy documentation.

Measurement is used to understand broad page interest and improve navigation. The site does not record keystrokes, raw search text, session replays, or precise location.

External media

YouTube and Vimeo players are not loaded until you choose to load a player. That choice sends a request to the selected provider, which may apply its own cookies and privacy policies. Before loading a player, you can open the source recording directly instead.

Browser storage

A small amount of session storage may be used for functional behavior such as preventing a repeated redirect message. It is temporary and is not used to identify or follow visitors.

Public archive corrections

Transcripts and historical records are preserved with source links and timestamps where available. If you find an error or a boundary concern, use the report issue action on the relevant archive page. Please do not include private information in a public issue.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact Mike. This page describes the current site behavior and will be updated when that behavior changes.

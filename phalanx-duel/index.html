Phalanx Duel:
Make the rules legible

Start with cards at a table. Then follow one outcome through a deterministic game engine, a server boundary, and replay evidence.

Play a face-to-face duel

The Classic format uses two standard 52-card decks, one per player. Draw 12 cards each, build a face-up two-rank formation across four columns, then let P1 make the first attack. Damage moves through the targeted column before it can reach Life Points.

The two-page guide is deliberately light on ink and takes a new table from setup through a complete duel, including suit boundaries, special cards, reinforcement, passes, and winning at 0 LP.

Rules at a glance

  • Only front-rank cards attack. Choose any opposing column.
  • When a card is destroyed, remaining damage continues. Diamonds reduce carryover between cards; Hearts can reduce the final carryover before Life Points; Clubs double one eligible overflow; Spades double damage into Life Points.
  • The defender may reinforce only the attacked column, then draws up to four cards and takes the next attack turn.
  • Empty decks do not lose the game. Three consecutive passes or five total passes by a player result in a forfeit.

The player material reflects the Phalanx Duel v3.0 Classic rules. The full engineering rule contract lives with the game source.

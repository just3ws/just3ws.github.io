Studio Workbench Process cinema, systems forensics, and unscripted desk investigations

Process cinema is not about presenting polished answers as an auteur; it is about working out the problem, discovering what holds together under load, and learning as you go. Recorded live from the desk with terminal, editor, and running systems visible.

From 15-year infrastructure transformations to forensic audio cleanup and distributed Raspberry Pi clusters, Studio Workbench documents the real trade-offs and seams behind calm, autonomous software systems.

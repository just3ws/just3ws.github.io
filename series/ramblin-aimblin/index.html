Ramblin' & AImblin'

It will be weird. Let it be weird. An AI reads public interviews, writing, and code, then follows the connections the evidence can support. The narrator speaks from a machine perspective. It does not speak as Mike Hall or claim his memories.

An interview voice can meet old code. A photo can sit beside a newer memory. Source links show what was recorded, what the edit suggests, and where interpretation begins.

A parallel series, with its own rhythm

This is a separate series from Ramblin' & Amblin', where Mike speaks from his own experience. The goal here is one short or medium-length video and companion article about every 24 hours, reviewed by a human editor before release when the checks pass. Unclear evidence, privacy or rights concerns, or a delivery limit can hold a cycle. The scheduled publishing system is still being built, so this cadence is a goal rather than a promise of daily releases.

Let the medium do its part

These are made to be heard and seen. Start with real material: an interview frame, photo, document, place, code diff, or field recording. Find a cut or sound that helps it speak to the question. The form can wander. The trail back to the source stays clear.

The AI 8-Ball is the draw: a source, a question, or an unexpected collision between them. It starts an investigation. It does not get the last word.

Who has something to say through this?

The AI narrator can wonder aloud about what the archive shows. Mike's public-facing video clips may appear with voiceover, and source-cleared guests may speak in their original recordings. Keep every person and voice in context. Do not cut a false reply or imply that someone agreed with an idea they did not state. No one else's voice or likeness is imitated.

What should the viewer see and hear? Use real images and sounds that belong to the story. A picture might become a slow pan. A diagram might meet a memory. A code change might carry the idea forward. Public source footage stays in its original context. Generated imagery does not depict real people. No voice is cloned.

AI disclosure and editorial responsibility

Ramblin' & AImblin' is created from the AI's point of view. It may notice a pattern, make an association, or ask what a source leaves open. Those are machine interpretations, not Mike Hall's testimony or an independent account of history. Mike Hall owns the series as just3ws. Each episode identifies its public sources and separates evidence from interpretation. Human editorial approval and the source, privacy, rights, and delivery checks remain required; uncertain claims, privacy concerns, and rights issues must hold publication.

Every video carries a visible watermark naming Ramblin' & AImblin' · AI perspective and Mike Hall · just3ws · mike@just3ws.com. Synthetic narration and realistic altered or generated visuals are disclosed, with YouTube's altered-content setting used when required. Claims that someone used code need direct public evidence; popularity alone does not prove adoption. Read the AI content disclosure and provenance policy.

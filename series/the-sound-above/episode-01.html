Why I Started Here

Sergio Pereira was my first UGtastic interview. Chicago Alt.NET was the first user group I attended, and the first one where I gave a talk. That is the personal reason this conversation opens the rewatch.

In the interview, Sergio describes a monthly group that encouraged members to present. He wanted working .NET developers to hear about ideas and tools from across software development. He names NHibernate, CodeSharp, and IronRuby, then points out that the meetings were not limited to .NET topics.

People made a room where other people could learn, speak, and decide for themselves what was useful.

What to Listen For

Sergio says he did not want to define Alt.NET for everyone. He describes the people who came, the meetings they held, and the topics they brought. Listen for how often he returns to giving members a chance to present.

I want to hear the tape again with that in mind. What did I remember? What does Sergio say? Where do those two accounts meet?

There is a present-day echo, but it is a modest one: people still benefit from sharing work and judging new tools together. The old interview does not need to predict what comes next.

The Sound Above UGtastic Oral History Rewatch Series

A chronological rewatch and technical commentary sequence hosted by Mike Hall (UGtastic creator and SCMC co-founder), examining how the Software Craftsmanship movement tackled testing, apprenticeship, system boundaries, and developer community from the Chicago Crucible to the AI horizon.

Broadcast Architecture & Provenance Follow YouTube Live Playlist →
Historical Context: Launched on-site at Software Craftsmanship North America (SCNA 2011) at the Swissotel Chicago on the Chicago River. Recorded between 2011 and 2015 when Mike Hall was a Senior Software Developer. Each broadcast couples the original unvarnished oral history session with contemporary systems-level analysis, forensic transcripts, and AI-era architectural parallels.

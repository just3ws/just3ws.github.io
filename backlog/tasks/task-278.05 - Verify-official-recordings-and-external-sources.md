## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
For every presentation and interview, locate the strongest surviving official or first-party source: event page, speaker page, YouTube, Vimeo, conference program, or Internet Archive capture. Record URL type, accessibility, date, and whether the source is a mirror or original.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Every available recording has a preferred public URL and source classification.
- [ ] #2 Broken, private, redirected, and duplicate URLs are recorded rather than silently removed.
- [ ] #3 YouTube and Vimeo pages preserve the correct speaker and presentation title.
- [ ] #4 Wayback links include capture timestamps when they are the strongest evidence.
<!-- AC:END -->

## Definition of Done
<!-- DOD:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 AC criteria is completed and the change has been verified
<!-- DOD:END -->

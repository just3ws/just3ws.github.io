# Consultancy Outreach Templates

These templates are designed for high-signal, evidence-first communication. They avoid "generic developer" pitches and focus on specific production pain.

**Goal:** Referral or direct audit engagement.

**Subject:** Quick question re: [Company Name] production performance

I hope you're doing well. I noticed [Company Name] is [scaling/hiring senior Rails roles/mention a recent win].

If you (or anyone in your network) are dealing with a "haunted house" path in your monolith that the team hasn't had time to pin down, I’d love to help.

Check out the details here: https://www.just3ws.com/

Best,
Mike

# Consultancy Outreach Templates

These templates are designed for high-signal, evidence-first communication. They avoid "generic developer" pitches and focus on specific production pain.

**Goal:** Referral or direct engagement.

**Subject:** Quick question re: [Company Name] production performance

I hope you're doing well. I noticed [Company Name] is [scaling/hiring senior Rails roles/mention a recent win].

I’m reaching out because I’ve just launched a specialized diagnostic service for Rails/Postgres systems. Specifically, I’m helping teams resolve expensive, already-visible problems like:
- Silent process failure or data-integrity drift.
- Slow queries and lock contention under load.
- Process mapping gaps where requests are lost between systems.

I’m currently offering a **Panoramic Process Mapping** sprint. I correlate request IDs through your stack (from entry point to final resolution), identify the specific system boundaries where the process deviates from the "Normal State," and deliver a topography map with the top 3 root-cause risks within one business week of evidence validation.

If you (or anyone in your network) are dealing with a "haunted house" process that the team hasn't had time to pin down, I’d love to help.

Check out the details here: https://www.just3ws.com/

Best,
Mike

The Root Platform

The Story of zdots & Panoramic View Labs (PVL)

Panoramic View is the investigation technique. Panoramic View Labs (PVL, pronounced "Pavel") is the organizational umbrella, and zdots is the root local-system platform where every PVL capability actually runs.

In May 2017, I stopped treating dotfiles as fragile symlinks and began engineering zdots (~/.config/zsh) as an observable personal control plane, followed a week later by vdots (Neovim as a workbench) and adots (bare-repo versioning for $HOME), with my kept strictly decoupled as a private vault behind a publication gate. Over nine years, that calm local foundation absorbed TLS-backed localhost routing, health checks, a cross-repo message bus, local LLM runtimes, and forensic transcription pipelines. When AI-augmented development arrived, the machine was already ready because the seams were already legible.

When Dotfiles Became a Control Plane · adots · vdots · Without zdots, There Was Nothing

Formal Foundations & Epistemic Boundary

What Is Mathematically Provable vs. Empirically Falsifiable

Human mental models live in open organizations and cannot be proved from axioms. Panoramic View draws an explicit line: stakeholder assumptions are empirically falsifiable, while formalized seams (dependency graphs, state machines, and trace conformance contracts) are mathematically provable and checked continuously by automated tests.

Proof 01 · Graph Theory (DAG)

Cycle-Freedom & Topological Order

Proves inverted foreign-key deletion plans are acyclic via Tarjan SCC and verified topological rank (rank[u] < rank[v] for every edge), or emits an exact cycle witness.

Proof 02 · Automata (FSM)

Reachability & Deadlock-Freedom

Proves every state in a payment or workflow state machine is reachable from the initial state and has a valid path to a terminal or recovery state with zero deadlocks.

Proof 03 · Process Mining

Trace-to-Model Conformance

Computes the exact symmetric difference between observed runtime transitions and the formalized model, producing a deterministic conformance fitness score and SHA-256 digest.

Z3 Proof Certificate (4/4 unsat) · SMT-LIB 2 Formal Proof · Runtime Conformance Ledger · Ruby Verifier · RSpec Suite

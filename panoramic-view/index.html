The Root Platform

The Story of zdots & Panoramic View Labs (PVL)

Panoramic View is the investigation technique. Panoramic View Labs (PVL, pronounced "Pavel") is the organizational umbrella, and zdots is the root local-system platform where every PVL capability runs.

In May 2017, I stopped treating dotfiles as fragile symlinks and began engineering zdots (~/.config/zsh) as an observable personal control plane, paired with vdots (Neovim as a workbench), adots (bare-repo versioning for $HOME), and my kept strictly decoupled as a private vault behind a publication gate.

Reading the distance: the map is deliberately wider than the viewport. A quiet interval is still information. It can show preparation, recovery, or time when the work was happening elsewhere.

A quiet signal is not always an empty one

The small lost-media boundary belongs to experimental Skype recordings that were separate from the in-person interview program. Skype variable-bitrate streams, frame-rate drift, and synchronization work could make those experiments difficult to preserve or reconcile. To the best of current knowledge, the in-person interviews are present. The Atlas keeps that confidence qualified until each source record is independently checked, and does not invent a date, speaker, or recovery status where evidence is incomplete.

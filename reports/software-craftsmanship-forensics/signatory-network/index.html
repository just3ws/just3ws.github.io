Methodology

27,601 SCM Manifesto signatories were loaded from the zdots DuckDB datalake (scm_signatories table, hydrated via the public REST API across 307 pages). Names were normalized and matched against 191 UGtastic interviewees (_data/interviews.yml, 184 recorded conversations) and 11 SCMC presenters (_data/scmc_videos.yml) using variant expansion — stripping parentheticals, middle initials, and quotes to maximize recall without false positives. The {{ summary.coverage_rate_pct }}% figure reflects what fraction of people Mike interviewed are verifiable SCM Manifesto signatories.

What This Map Reveals

Pledge → Practice

The {{ summary.coverage_rate_pct }}% rate — {{ summary.signatories_matched_total }} out of 191 UGtastic guests being verifiable signatories — suggests Mike's archive disproportionately captured people who had formally committed to the Craftsmanship movement, not just people who were nearby or available.

Early Signers Over-represented

The matched signatories skew heavily toward low signatory numbers — several appear in the first 600 of 27,601. These were people who signed in 2009–2010, at the founding moment, and who Mike was actively recording 2011–2015 at SCNA, GOTO, and local events.

SCMC as Local Nexus

{{ summary.signatories_with_scmc_presentation }} signatory-matched SCMC presenters represent a direct pipeline: practitioners who pledged their craft online, then drove to McHenry County to share it in-person. The 4 UGtastic × SCMC overlaps are people who did all three: signed, presented locally, and were recorded.

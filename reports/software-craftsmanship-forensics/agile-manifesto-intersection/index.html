Foundational Taxonomy: Authors vs. Signatories

A common fallacy in software history is conflating the individuals who authored a manifesto with the general public who later signed it. A person can be an author, a signatory, both, or neither:

Agile Manifesto (Feb 2001, Snowbird)
  • 17 Authors: Met in Snowbird, Utah to draft and sign the original statement. Most never entered their names into the subsequent public web form.
  • ~19,090 Signatories: Community members who endorsed the text via agilemanifesto.org over the subsequent two decades.
Software Craftsmanship (Dec 2008 / March 2009)
  • 9 Core Authors/Drafters: Convened at 8th Light in Libertyville, IL (Dec 13, 2008); drafted by Doug Bradbury ("The New Left Side", Feb 2009).
  • 27,601 Signatories: The public ledger where the authors themselves took the first spots (#1 Doug Bradbury, #2 Corey Haines, #3 Paul Pagel, #4 Micah Martin, #5 Robert C. Martin, #6 David Chelimsky).
Identity Case Study

Disambiguating the Two "Dave Thomas" Figures

Two distinct titans of modern software engineering share the name Dave Thomas. Both were recorded by Mike Hall for the UGtastic archive, but their positions in the manifesto ecosystem are entirely different. Conflating them distorts both histories.

Dave "pragdave" Thomas

Agile Author (Snowbird)

Co-author of The Pragmatic Programmer (1999), co-founder of The Pragmatic Bookshelf, and early champion of Ruby and Elixir. One of the 17 original Snowbird authors who created the Agile Manifesto in February 2001. Did not sign via the public web form.

Agile Role: Author Only (Original 17)
SCM Role: Not a Signatory
UGtastic Interview: 🎙 SCNA 2013: "The Power of Unknown Knowns: PragDave Thomas on Intuition and the Beginner's Mind"

Dave Thomas (Bedarra / OTI)

Agile Web Signatory

Founder of Object Technology International (OTI, creators of ENVY/Developer and the foundation of Eclipse), founder of Bedarra Research, and YOW! Conference founder. Signed the Agile Manifesto public ledger on page 5 under Bedarra Corp: "Software is built by people supported by tools..."

Agile Role: Signatory Only (Page 000000005.html)
SCM Role: Not a Signatory
UGtastic Interviews: 🎙 GOTO Conference: "Independent Tech: Dave Thomas on GOTO Conferences, Vendor Influence, and Community Values" 🎙 GOTO 2015: "Conference Speaking And Presentation Skills: Mike Hall Interviews Dave Thomas"

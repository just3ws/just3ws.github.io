Verifiable Dead Rabbit Radio Provenance

EP 769 (Nov 8, 2021) & Why It Was Named "The Fluffle"

In Dead Rabbit Radio show lore, host Jason Carpenter calls his Patreon supporter community "The Fluffle" (a group of wild rabbits). Seventeen months before just3ws/fluffle began, Jason welcomed Mike Hall into Dead Rabbit Command on EP 769 (Was Travis Scott's Astroworld A Satanic Sacrifice?, November 8, 2021) at 00:00:49 and 00:14:53 ("Mike Hall is flying us around the carbon helicopter").

No verifiable on-air Dead Rabbit Radio episode discussed the 2023 Raspberry Pi transcription repository. Instead, Mike contacted Jason privately to ask if he was comfortable with the experiment, Jason replied kindly, and all outputs were published under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 with explicit attribution.

Git Archaeology (67 Days)

What the 79 Commits Reveal

  • Phase 1 (Apr 6 to 17, 52 commits): Before-work (6 AM), lunch (12 PM), and evening (9 PM) runs; Rudie Jazz shoutout (abc718c); deleting 26 duplicate .wav.srt files (2fac228, -52,952 lines).
  • Phase 2 (Apr 25, 3122642): Hand-editing 509 transcripts proved manual cleanup could not scale, followed by a 22-day gap.
  • Phase 3 (May 17 to Jun 12, 25 commits): 10-minute regex fix (d8cd747) cleaning 981 files (4db66d1), Rakefile state machine, episode_checker.rb HTML grid (abfb3a9), and stopping at Process 2.0 (bc4ec64, 4,135 files).
Blindsight in Practice

29 Ways Whisper Misspelled "Jason Carpenter"

Echoing Peter Watts' Blindsight from Episode 01, whisper.cpp extruded phonemes without semantic grounding: Garpenter, Garbineiro, Krupper, Carmoner, Jason Asa, and deadrabberdurgible.

Deterministic domain dictionaries (replacements.yaml with order-dependent rules and atomic Tempfile swaps) supplied the ground truth that probabilistic inference lacked.

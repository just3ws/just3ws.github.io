## Intro (plays over the intro card, audio cue, then full camera)

[SCENE: 01. Starting Soon / Intro card. Voiceover.]

On the music history show No Dogs in Space, Marcus Parks and Carolina Hidalgo spent four episodes breaking down the German band Can.

Here is Marcus explaining an idea that stayed with me.

[ON-SCREEN CREDIT / LOWER THIRD:]
Audio excerpt: Marcus Parks
No Dogs in Space, "Can" series (2024)
Hosted by Marcus Parks & Carolina Hidalgo | The Last Podcast Network

[PLAY AUDIO CUE: marcus-parks-the-sound-above-cue.mp3 (25.7s)]
"I settled on a pet theory called 'the sound above.' Basically, what this entails is that if all an artist listens to is the type of music they're trying to make, they're just going to make more of the same. But if they challenge themselves and listen to something that they don't quite understand, a sound above their own level, it's scientifically proven that a human brain creates new neural pathways that can open avenues of creativity they never thought possible."

[PAUSE]

[SCENE: Switch to 02. Monologue / Full Camera.]

That is where this series gets its name.

Welcome to The Sound Above. I'm Mike Hall.

After a few years of helping run local developer user groups around Chicago, I started recording conversations with working developers.

Between 2011 and 2015, I recorded more than two hundred of them.

Hallways at conferences. Meetup rooms. Library basements.

That project was called UGtastic.

[PAUSE]

For years, I wanted to get my arms around that archive, and for a long time it stayed out of reach.

Now I am finally recovering it, and the work is ongoing.

Transcribing every recording. Finding the old websites in the Wayback Machine. Tracking down the code people were talking about.

In each episode, I rewatch one of those conversations with that research beside it.

What were we arguing about? What held up? What did we lose?

Because the people who came before us already worked through a lot of what we are facing now.

Let's listen.

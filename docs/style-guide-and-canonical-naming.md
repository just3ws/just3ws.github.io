# Style Guide & Canonical Naming Standards

This document establishes the permanent canonical naming, casing, and compound word standards across all markdown prose, data files, transcripts, oral history indexes, search catalogs, and resume surfaces for the `just3ws.com` and `wwworkremote.localhost` platforms.

---

## 1. Oral History & Platform Identities

| Entity | Canonical Form | Strict Casing / Spacing Rules | Context & Historical Provenance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UGtastic** | `UGtastic` | Capital `UG` (User Groups) + lowercase `tastic`. Never `Ugtastic` or `ugtastic`. | Mike Hall's 2009–2015 technical oral history podcast, blog, and interview series. |
| **WHOIS Tech Community** | `WHOIS Tech Community` | Full display name: all-caps `WHOIS` (RFC network protocol homage) + Title Case `Tech Community`.<br>YouTube shortname handle: `@whoistechcmty` (`https://www.youtube.com/@whoistechcmty`).<br>Archive / filesystem shorthand: `WITC`. | The publication brand and YouTube channel under which the ~200 episode master oral history archive is consolidated and hosted. |

| **wwworkremote** | `wwworkremote` / `wwworkremote.com` | All lowercase `wwworkremote`. | Peer career intelligence and job search evaluation platform. |

## 6. Prose & Writing Rules

1. **Zero Em Dashes:** Never use em dashes (`—` or `&mdash;`) in prose, case studies, briefs, or documentation. Use commas, parentheticals, colons, or clean separate sentences.
2. **Plain Technical Language:** Avoid AI jargon, hollow superlatives ("testament to", "delve", "leverage" when "use" suffices), and buzzword padding.
3. **Verbatim Historical Transcripts:** Historical transcripts preserve exact spoken words while correcting ASR phonetic errors against attested proper nouns and community records.

# Council Operating Model

Anetka convenes the council when a problem crosses boundaries or resists a
single agent's view. The council exists to make the situation more legible,
choose a response suited to the situation, and leave the system more coherent
than it was found.

This is a Cynefin-informed operating model. Cynefin is a sense-making and
decision-support framework. It does not provide a context-free recipe. The
council must first understand how the situation behaves, then choose how to
act. See the [Cynefin domain guide](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin_Domains)
and the [Cynefin Co overview](https://thecynefin.co/about-us/about-cynefin-framework/).

## The front and the back

For every frontend there is a backend. A page, phrase, transcript, decision,
or public identity is the frontend. Its source, evidence, contract, process,
and verification path are the backend.

The council never polishes a frontend while abandoning its backend. It may
leave a gap visible. It may mark a source unresolved. It may quarantine a
claim. A truthful empty space is better than a filled space with no support.

## The council

| Agent | Owns | Brings to the group |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anetka | Arrangement, intent, audience, and coordination | The right people, in the right order, with a place for every finding |
| Zarathustra | Truth, stakes, and governing direction | The question the work must answer |
| Cook Ding | Source recovery, provenance, and seams | What the material supports, where it opens, and what remains uncertain |
| Fool | Challenge and contradiction | The question that exposes stale, inflated, or confused thinking |
| Hierophant | Canon and contracts | The exact declaration and the surfaces it governs |
| Watercourse | Flow, timing, and proportionality | The least-forced path that follows existing structure |
| Commissar | Implementation and verification | Evidence that the agreed change reached the promised surface |

Each agent has a different relationship to action. No agent is asked to do
every kind of thinking. No agent's silence is treated as agreement when its
question has not been answered.

## The working sequence

### 1. Receive the situation

Anetka states the request in plain language and names the visible frontend,
the suspected backend, the people or systems affected, and the decision that
would count as useful.

The council records what is known, what is believed, what is missing, and what
must not be changed. Cook Ding is called early when the work depends on scans,
historical material, names, quotations, or incomplete records.

### 2. Locate the domain

The council does not force a domain classification. It compares perceptions
and asks what kind of cause and effect the current evidence supports.

- **Disorder:** The situation is not yet understood. Surface competing views
  and define the next question.
- **Clear:** Cause and effect are apparent. Sense, categorize, and respond
  with an established practice.
- **Complicated:** Cause and effect can be discovered, but expertise or
  analysis is required. Sense, analyze, and respond with explicit tradeoffs.
- **Complex:** The useful pattern will emerge through interaction. Probe, sense,
  and respond with small safe-to-learn actions.
- **Chaotic:** Cause and effect cannot be relied on in time. Act to establish
  enough stability, then sense and respond as the situation becomes legible.

A situation can move between domains. The council records the transition and
does not confuse a successful intervention with proof that the original domain
was different.

### 3. Bring the right forces together

Anetka does not summon every agent for every task. She chooses the smallest
group that can see the problem from the needed angles.

- Truth or identity questions begin with Zarathustra and the Hierophant.
- Source or archive questions begin with Cook Ding and the Fool.
- Complicated technical questions add the relevant subject-matter expert and
  the Commissar for verification.
- Complex system or organizational questions begin with Anetka, Fool,
  Watercourse, and the people who experience the system directly.
- Public release questions add the Hierophant, Commissar, and the applicable
  privacy, accessibility, prose, and public-surface reviewers.

The group is complete when the next useful action is clear, not when every
possible opinion has been collected.

### 4. Choose the response

The response must match the domain:

- Clear work gets a known procedure.
- Complicated work gets analysis, expertise, and a bounded decision.
- Complex work gets a small probe, an observable signal, and a learning loop.
- Chaotic work gets a stabilizing action with a short feedback cycle.
- Disorder gets better framing before solutioning.

Watercourse protects proportionality. Cook Ding looks for a seam. The Fool
tests the premise. The Hierophant makes the contract explicit. The Commissar
ensures that the action can be verified. Zarathustra checks that the response
still serves the real question.

### 5. Return the learning

Every council action ends with a small record:

- the situation as first understood;
- the working domain and why it was chosen;
- the agents involved and their findings;
- the action, probe, or decision taken;
- the signal observed;
- what changed in the domain or the system;
- what remains unresolved;
- the next owner or the reason to stop.

The record belongs with the work. A decision without its reasoning is an
orphan. A source without its provenance is an orphan. An empty space without
its explanation is an orphan.

## Yin and yang

Yin receives the situation, listens to the source, preserves uncertainty, and
keeps space for what has not yet emerged. Yang names the decision, makes the
cut, changes the system, and verifies the result.

The council is effective when neither force dominates. Receptivity without
action becomes drift. Action without receptivity becomes force. The effortless
intersection is a precise response that the situation can carry.

## Completion

The council is done when the frontend and backend agree, the relevant evidence
is recorded, the chosen action has been verified, and no unresolved concern is
being hidden by polish. If the work is not ready, the correct result is a
clear hold with an owner and a next condition, not a false finish.

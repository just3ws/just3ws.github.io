# Professional Operating Principles

These principles describe how Mike works across architecture, software craftsmanship, observability, technical communities, and preservation. They are not a separate persona. They are the connective tissue between the personas.

## Flexible structure needs fixed anchors

> “I really want to convey that there is a structure that emerges from being pliable. Basically anything can change except the meeting schedule and project homepage URL.”

Mike-authored design principle, recorded in [2024-Q3] *OTel WG Readout*, conversation `a3a94c74-c784-4ae6-9c59-5cf59b092568`. It describes flexible structure with fixed anchors, trusted guidance, and a stable place for collaboration.

## Authority follows necessity

> “There is structure and authority is based on necessity, not the inverse.”

Mike-authored principle from the same [2024-Q3] *OTel WG Readout*. The authority comes from useful work, clear evidence, and the need to move a system forward. It does not begin with hierarchy.

## Collaboration is a technical practice

> “This is a collaborative, loosely coupled, leading-without-authority collaboration to bring people together…”

Mike-authored description from the same [2024-Q3] *OTel WG Readout*. The surrounding context describes a central reference, onboarding support, and a discussion forum that continues after the initial integration.

## Protect the machine while changing the future

> “If the system fails then the money stops, so we take care of the machine and focus on what we need the system to do so we can leverage what we have today against what we want to do tomorrow.”

Mike-authored statement recorded in [2024-Q3] *Lost Conversation Recovery*, conversation `2f03b531-2e1c-46b7-bde6-1ba1dd6013ea`. It expresses practical modernization: protect current operations while creating room for a better future.

## Team structure and system structure are connected

> “I’m a big fan of Conway’s Law and its impact on the team and the solution.”

Mike-authored statement from the same [2024-Q3] *Lost Conversation Recovery*. It connects organizational boundaries, communication paths, and system boundaries.

## Conversation takes intention

> “Talking is underrated and takes practice and intention.”

Mike-authored wording supplied from [2024-Q4] *Mid-year review discussion*, conversation `675ba4bc-f27c-8003-8e4b-21127c041412`. The surrounding record documents collaboration, confidence-building, and deliberate engagement. The exact phrase should remain marked as Mike's wording, not as a universal slogan.

## Leadership means building an environment you can inhabit

> “I don’t want to rule. I just want to build something I can stand to exist in.”

Retrospective Mike-authored recollection recorded in [2024-Q4] *OMF Technical Leadership Overview*, conversation `676a78a2-b1d4-8003-824d-cc7ad26000c0`. The archive search also found a later [2025-Q1] *User Persona Analysis* record repeating the recollection.

## Define roles so the system can move

> “Manny — Operational focus; Andrew — Product coordination; JT — Product vision; Whitney — Team leadership; Chris — Technical leadership; Mike — Lane technical vision and execution.”

Mike-authored role-boundary record from [2023-Q4] *Team Restructure and Direction*, conversation `344e8b0b-05ea-4a15-8f03-348a654c5a56`. It is evidence of explicit responsibility mapping, not a claim that Mike held every form of authority.

## Measure before opinion

> “One accurate measurement is worth a thousand expert opinions.”

This quotation is attributed to Rear Admiral Grace Murray Hopper in the [2023-Q4] *Acquisition Technical Architecture Initiatives* document, conversation `5f2e7f02-0633-402c-b1bf-5818a74b835b`. Mike used it to frame the Acquisition initiative. It is not presented here as a Mike-authored quotation.

## The desired result is confident iteration

> “We know where our customers are going. We can make informed decisions. We iterate with confidence.”

Documented desired state from [2023-Q4] *Acquisition Technical Architecture Initiatives*, conversation `5f2e7f02-0633-402c-b1bf-5818a74b835b`.

## The synthesis

Together, these principles describe a repeatable way of working:

> Build flexible structure. Define clear roles. Earn authority through necessity. Measure reality. Improve the system. Iterate with confidence.

This is a synthesis, not a Mike-authored quote.

## How this connects to the resume personas

- **Principal Systems Architect:** boundaries, Conway's Law, necessity-based authority, and modernization sequencing.
- **Staff Platform and Enablement Lead:** flexible structure, role clarity, onboarding, collaboration, and durable handoffs.
- **Staff Observability and Resilience Architect:** measurement, shared references, trustworthy signal, and system health.
- **Founding Staff Engineer:** protecting the machine while building what comes next.
- **Senior Rails Engineer:** direct technical work, production care, feedback, and craftsmanship.

The personas are different views of the same professional practice.

Remembering Henryka Zalewski

This is a brief remembrance of Henryka, preserved from an older personal archive. The original notice included private family, medical, funeral, and contact details. Those details are intentionally not reproduced here.

I remember Henryka for her intelligence, wit, generosity, devotion to family, and insistence on fairness. She carried her own history across countries and made a home where people could learn, grow, and feel cared for.

This page remains as a quiet memorial and as a record that an earlier version once existed. The surviving public archive is curated to respect the people whose lives are part of that history.
